(9) **Reasons for more time:** cross-references added to completing a program, beginning a new one, and OPT/STEM (214.2(f)(5)(i)). (11) **Public high school:** may include charter or other taxpayer-funded schools (214.2(f)(5)(i)(C)). (12) **Readmission after a change of status:** 248.1(e) no longer refers to forms accompanying the approved change of status.
