## Q6

**Answer:**
Under 351.701(a), a released competitive-service employee with a current rating of record of Level 2 or higher must be offered assignment to a competitive position that requires no reduction, or the least reduction, in representative rate. The position must be in the same competitive area, have the same type of work schedule and be one he is qualified for. Under 351.701(b), it must be held by an employee with lower retention standing in the same tenure group and be no more than three grades (or grade intervals) below his own. The limit is five grades for a preference eligible with a compensable service-connected disability of 30 percent or more.
(a) Jordan, a GS-13 in one-grade interval progression with a Level 2 rating, has assignment rights to:
- position 1 (GS-12, Ellis);
- position 2 (GS-11, Frank);
- position 3 (GS-10, Gale).
The agency must offer position 1, the GS-12 held by Ellis, which is the least possible reduction.
The others are not available:
- Position 4 (GS-9, Hana) is four grades down, beyond the three-grade limit.
- Position 5 (Ito) is held by someone with higher standing.
- Position 6 (Kim) is part-time and does not match his full-time schedule.
- Position 7 (Lund) is in a different competitive area.
(b) Preference eligible with 30%+ disability: the limit becomes five grades, so position 4 (GS-9, Hana) also becomes an assignment right. The offer is still position 1 (GS-12, Ellis). Positions 5, 6 and 7 stay unavailable.
(c) Level 1: 351.701(a) requires a current rating of Level 2 or higher, so he has no assignment rights to any of the seven positions. This is the text's gap-free reading; it does not itself say what happens next beyond 'rather than furlough or separation' no longer being required.
(d) Excepted service: 351.701 applies to a 'competitive service tenure group employee'. 351.705(a) says an agency 'may, at its discretion, adopt provisions' giving excepted-service employees assignment rights under the same appointing authority on the same basis as 351.701. So there is no assignment right as a matter of the regulation. Any right depends on an agency policy the documents do not show.
(e) The 2026 text contains no rule that a Level 2 employee cannot take a position held by a higher-rated person. The only conditions are the released employee's current rating of Level 2 or higher (351.701(a)) and the holder's lower retention standing in the same tenure group (351.701(b)(1)). The text uses only 'assignment' and 'displacing'; it has no 'bumping' or 'retreating' categories. The preamble says the rule replaces 'bump and retreat' concepts with assignment to a position held by a lower-standing employee (91 FR 49178-79, item 12) and eliminates multiple rounds of bump and retreat (91 FR 49198).
