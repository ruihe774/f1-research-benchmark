## The rules in brief

**Which rules apply** (DATES, 91 FR 49178; implementation section, 91 FR 49206–49207): "An agency that issued a RIF notice before the effective date of the rule must process the RIF under the regulations in effect when the RIF notice was issued. An agency that issues a RIF notice on or after the effective date must apply the RIF provisions as amended by this final rule." The test is the date the agency issues the notice, against September 2, 2026. It is not in the regulatory text.

**Order of retention** (351.501): separate registers for the competitive service and excepted service tenure groups; within a tenure group, by performance credit "as augmented by veterans' preference", in descending order; ties broken by tenure subgroup (I ahead of II), then by the earliest service computation date; if still tied, "the agency may select any tied employee for release" (351.601(c)).

**Performance credit** (351.503): Level 5 = 7, Level 4 = 5, Level 3 = 3, Level 2 = 0, Level 1 = 0; sum of the three most recent ratings of record "received during the 4-year period prior to the date of issuance of reduction in force notices" (or prior to the cutoff date if the agency uses one). Two ratings: proxy = average of the two values (may end in .5); credit = the two values plus the proxy. One rating: its value times three. None: "based on the modal rating as defined in § 351.203" (no formula given; the definition is not printed).

**Veterans' preference** (351.504(a)): +5 for a preference eligible with a compensable service-connected disability of 30 percent or more (subgroup AD); +3 for every other preference eligible (subgroup A); 0 for others (subgroup B). The subgroups are labels on the register and in the notice; they are not a ranking factor or tie-breaker. Retired members (351.504(b)): preference eligible only if (1) the retirement is based on disability from armed conflict or an instrumentality of war, (2) retired pay "is not based upon 20 or more years of full-time active service", or (3) continuous covered employment since November 30, 1964; and, under (4), a retiree at the rank of major or above must also be a disabled veteran and meet one of (1) to (3).

**Tenure subgroups** (351.502(a)): subgroup I, career employees not serving an initial probationary period "as of the date the employee receives a specific reduction in force notice"; subgroup II, every other competitive service employee not serving an initial probationary period or a temporary appointment of one year or less. Employees serving an initial probationary period are not competing employees (351.202(d)).

**Retention standing date** (351.507(a)): "determined as of the date the employee receives a specific reduction in force notice".

## Q5. Which rules apply, and when standing is fixed (9 pts)

(a)(i) Notice issued August 28, 2026: **the earlier rules apply**, although the RIF takes effect October 27 (DATES, 91 FR 49178). The final rule describes them (91 FR 49186): performance credits are "averaged, rounded, and expressed as additional years of service. Level 5 ratings add 20 years, Level 4 ratings add 16 years, and Level 3 ratings add 12 years", with the preamble's own example of an employee with 3/3/3 outranking one with 5/5/5. Lee: SCD 1998 with 12 years of credit, adjusted to **1986**. Park: SCD 2019 with 20 years, adjusted to **1999**. Same tenure group and veterans' subgroup, so the earlier adjusted date ranks higher: Lee is retained and **Park is released**.

(a)(ii) Notice issued September 2, 2026: "on or after the effective date", so the **amended rules** apply. Lee 3 + 3 + 3 = 9; Park 7 + 7 + 7 = 21. **Lee is released.**

(a)(iii) September 3, 2026: same as (ii). **Lee is released.**

(b) **No.** 351.805(b) (91 FR 49226): "An amended notice issued under this paragraph solely to state a later effective date is not a new specific reduction in force notice for purposes of § 351.507 and does not require the agency to redetermine or recalculate retention standing". Standing was fixed when the employees received their notices on September 3 (351.507(a)), and the October 15 rating is also after the issuance of notices, so it is outside 351.503(c)(1).

(c) Version of the rules: the date the agency **issues** the RIF notice, against September 2, 2026 (DATES; 91 FR 49206–49207). End of the four-year period: the **date of issuance** of RIF notices, or the agency's cutoff date if it uses one (351.503(c)(1)–(2)). Tenure subgroup and retention standing: the date the employee **receives** the specific RIF notice (351.502(a)(2)–(3); 351.507(a)).

## Q6. Assignment rights (7 pts)

(a) Jordan has an assignment right because his current annual rating of record is Level 2 or higher (351.701(a)). The right reaches positions held by an employee "with lower retention standing in the same tenure group", "not more than three grades" below his position, for which he is qualified, in the same competitive area and with the same type of work schedule (351.701(a)–(b)). Within reach: **positions 1, 2 and 3** (GS-12, GS-11, GS-10). The agency must offer the position requiring "no reduction, or the least possible reduction, in representative rate": **position 1, the GS-12 held by Ellis**. Position 4 (GS-9) is four grades down; position 5 is held by an employee with higher standing; position 6 has a different work schedule; position 7 is in another competitive area.

(b) The limit becomes **five grades** (351.701(b)(2)), so position 4 (GS-9) is also within reach. The offer is still position 1.

(c) **No assignment right** under 351.701: paragraph (a) covers only employees whose current annual rating of record is "minimally satisfactory (Level 2) or equivalent, or higher".

(d) No right under 351.701, which is for the competitive service tenure group. 351.705(a): an agency "may, at its discretion, adopt provisions that provide competing employees in the excepted service with assignment rights ... on the same basis". It depends on whether the agency has adopted such provisions.

(e) Both are gone. OPM eliminated former 351.701(d), the "restriction on employees with a current annual performance rating of record of Level 2 from obtaining assignment to positions held by employees with a higher performance rating" (91 FR 49204). It consolidated 351.701(b) and (c): "there is no longer a need for the separate concepts of an employee 'bumping' another employee in a lower subgroup, and 'retreating' to the position of a lower-ranked employee in the same subgroup"; instead there is a single assignment right to a position held by an employee with lower retention standing (91 FR 49204).

## Q8. Administrative leave during the notice period (7 pts)

(a) Adopted, not yet in force: **FR Doc. 2026-21320**, published October 19, 2026, **effective November 18, 2026**. Revised 351.806 (91 FR 65939): "(a) General. Under ordinary circumstances, an employee will remain in a duty status during the notice period. (b) Administrative leave. Nothing in this section precludes an agency from placing an employee on administrative leave for all or part of the notice period when the agency determines that such leave is appropriate and the use of administrative leave is consistent with part 630, subpart N, of this chapter." Paragraph (c) keeps the emergency lack-of-work-or-funds rule. Today, November 14, 2026, the earlier text applies: as the rulemaking describes it, agencies keep the employee in a duty status during the notice period "when possible" (91 FR 65933; proposed rule 2026-13073). The earlier section itself is not printed in full.

(b) Consent: **not needed.** 5 CFR 630.1403(c) lets an agency provide administrative leave "at its sole and exclusive discretion", and (c)(10) covers "employees subject to an involuntary separation via a reduction in force (RIF) during the period between receipt of the RIF notice and the RIF separation when the agency determines it is in the Government's best interests" (91 FR 65940); the preamble says leave may be provided "with or without the employee's consent" (91 FR 65933). Cap: **none is stated for this use; uncertain.** The 10-workday limit applies to investigative leave. The preamble says the governmentwide limit, "currently 12 weeks in any individual instance", applies to "the workforce-realignment uses in § 630.1403(c)(9) and (c)(11)" (91 FR 65927); paragraph (c)(10) carries no such limit in its text, and the general principle is that administrative leave is for short periods unless a paragraph (c) use applies. Here the leave runs October 26 to December 18, under 8 weeks, so it would be within 12 weeks in any case. The answer should say that the text does not state a cap for (c)(10), and note either that the rule is not yet in force or that whether the governmentwide limit reaches (c)(10) is not stated.

(c) OPM policy guidance: the "Template for Agency Administrative Leave Policies" (CPM 2025-12, July 30, 2025), which "already provided that an authorized agency official may provide administrative leave between an employee's receipt of a notice of involuntary separation and the effective date of that separation" (91 FR 65926).

(d) No. The new provisions "address only whether an employee may be placed in a paid, non-duty status during a RIF notice period. Those provisions do not alter the substantive or procedural requirements governing RIF ... Nothing in this rule authorizes an agency to bypass RIF competition, retention registers, or appeal rights" (91 FR 65926).

## Q10. The CTAP definition of "displaced" (5 pts)

(a) Only the introductory text: "(1) A current competitive service employee in the competitive service tenure group at grade GS-15 (or equivalent) or below (or another qualifying competitive service appointment, as determined by OPM) who:" with nothing after it. (b) **FR Doc. 2026-18800**, 91 FR 58363, published and effective **September 15, 2026**, revised paragraph (1) in full, restoring (1)(i) and (1)(ii). Reason for no notice and comment: good cause, because the document "makes no substantive change to the regulations. It restores regulatory text that was in effect before September 2, 2026, that OPM did not propose to remove, and that the final rule did not discuss removing"; effective on publication for the same reasons.

(c) Paragraph (1)(i): an employee who "Received a reduction in force (RIF) separation notice under part 351 of this chapter and has not declined an offer under part 351, subpart G, of this chapter of a position with the same type of work schedule and a representative rate at least as high as that of the position from which the employee will be separated". Declining a **lower**-rate position does not take her out: **still displaced**. Declining a position with the **same** representative rate and work schedule does: **not displaced** under (1)(i).
