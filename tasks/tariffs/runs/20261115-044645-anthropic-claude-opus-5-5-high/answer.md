## Q2

**Answer:**
**(a) Section 338 duty on each entry**
- Entry 1 (2:00 p.m., Aug 20): $0. This is before the operative start.
- Entry 2 (11:59 p.m., Aug 21): $0.
- Entry 3 (12:01 a.m., Aug 22): $40,000 × 50% = $20,000. The duty applies at or after 12:01 a.m. ET on Aug 22, 2026.
- Controlling text: clause (1) of Procl. 11056: "The effective date of the additional ad valorem duties imposed in Proclamations 11046, 11047, and 11048 shall be 12:01 a.m. eastern time on August 22, 2026."

**(b) How the start date was changed**
- Clauses (1) and (3) of 11046 and recital 9 still read "12:01 a.m. eastern time on August 19, 2026".
- Clause (1) of 11056 does two things: (i) it sets the effective date as Aug 22; and (ii) it amends only "the chapeau of Annex II" of each proclamation, deleting "August 19, 2026" and inserting "August 22, 2026".
- 11056 does **not** amend the numbered clauses (1) and (3) of 11046.
- The conflict is resolved by the first sentence of clause (1) of 11056 and by its clause (5): "Any provision of previous proclamations ... that is inconsistent with this proclamation is superseded to the extent of such inconsistency."
- 11056 was signed Aug 18, before the original date, but published only Aug 24.

**(c) Getting the deposit back**
- Clause (2) of 11056: agencies are to "as soon as practicable, suspend the collection" of the duties to the extent needed.
- Clause (4): "To the extent that implementation of this proclamation requires a refund of duties collected, refunds shall be processed pursuant to applicable law and CBP's standard procedures for such refunds."
- Nothing more specific is given.

**(d) Was a further presidential action needed?**
- No. 11056 suspended the duties only "for a period of 3 days"; the start date was fixed in its operative text.
- Later recitals (recitals 2–4 of 11061 and 11064; recitals 2–4 of 11076) say:
  - 11056 suspended the duties after Canada "expressed a commitment" to remove the discrimination;
  - "On August 21, 2026, Canada reneged on its commitment, ceased negotiating in good faith, and did not remove the discrimination";
  - "at 12:01 a.m. eastern time on August 22, 2026, the 3-day suspension ... lapsed, and the additional ad valorem duties ... became effective."
- These are recitals, not operative text.

**(e) Why Aug 19 was the original date**
- Section 338 duties may not take effect "earlier than 30 days after the President's proclamation" (recital 10 of 11046).
- Signed July 20: July 21–31 is 11 days, plus Aug 1–19 is 19 days, giving 30 days. So Aug 19 is the earliest permissible date.
- For comparison only: 30 days after publication (July 23) is Aug 22. The documents do not draw this link.

**Basis:**

- 11056 recitals 4–5 and clauses (1), (2), (4), (5) (91 FR 54790–91); FR Doc. 2026-17294.

## Q7

**Answer:**
**(a) France, 3¢/kg, 2,000 kg, value $12,000**
- MFN duty = 2,000 kg × $0.03 = $60.
- Ad valorem equivalent = $60 ÷ $12,000 = 0.5%.
- EU cap is 10% net of MFN: 10% − 0.5% = 9.5%.
- Section 301 = $12,000 × 9.5% = **$1,140**.

**(b) 4% + 25¢/kg, 4,000 kg, value $20,000**
- MFN duty = (4% × $20,000 = $800) + (4,000 × $0.25 = $1,000) = $1,800.
- Ad valorem equivalent = $1,800 ÷ $20,000 = 9%.
- **Italy (EU):** 10% − 9% = 1%. Section 301 = $20,000 × 1% = **$200**.
- **Japan:** 12.5% − 9% = 3.5%. Section 301 = $20,000 × 3.5% = **$700**.
- **Brazil:** flat 12.5%; the equivalent-rate rule does not apply to Brazil. Section 301 = $20,000 × 12.5% = **$2,500**.

**(c) Duty payable $2,200 on value $20,000**
- Ad valorem equivalent = $2,200 ÷ $20,000 = 11%.
- **Taiwan:** 11% is at or above the 10% cap, so section 301 = **$0**.
- **Switzerland:** 12.5% − 11% = 1.5%. Section 301 = $20,000 × 1.5% = **$300**.

**(d) Where the rules are stated**
- **Non-ad valorem rates:** U.S. note 52(k), first paragraph, in Annex I of the USTR notice (91 FR 47394). For any good of an EU member state, Japan, South Korea, Switzerland or Taiwan with a specific or compound column 1-General rate, "the ad valorem equivalent rate of duty ... shall be determined by dividing the amount of duty payable under column 1-General by the customs value".
- The last two paragraphs of note 52(k) address only rates *below* the cap (the sum becomes 10% or 12.5%).
- **Rates at or above the cap (section 301 duty of zero):** stated in Sec. 1(a)(ii) of the memorandum (91 FR 47720), in the notice preamble at sec. II.A (91 FR 47320–21), and in each "for greater certainty" sentence (EU 47323; Japan 47324; South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan 47327).
