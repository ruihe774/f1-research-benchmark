## Q11

**Answer:**
The principal discrepancies I found are:

1. **11056 amends only the Annex II chapeau, not the operative clauses.** 11056 clause (1) says the effective date “shall be” Aug. 22 and amends only “the chapeau of Annex II” to delete Aug. 19 and insert Aug. 22 (91 FR 54790). But 11046 clauses (1) and (3) still say Aug. 19 (91 FR 46641), as do 11047 and 11048 (91 FR 46654–55, 46665). The later clause (1) of 11056 is the operative fix, but the amended proclamations are internally inconsistent.

2. **The September/October recitals say the original duties were effective Aug. 19, while also saying they became effective Aug. 22.** 11061 recital 1 says “effective August 19, 2026,” but recital 4 says the suspension lapsed and the duties “became effective” at 12:01 a.m. Aug. 22 (91 FR 58311–12). The same pattern appears in 11062 (91 FR 58319–20), 11063 (91 FR 58325–26), 11064 (91 FR 58331–32), 11065 (91 FR 58339–40), and 11076 (91 FR 68861–62). The operative history is Aug. 22; the Aug. 19 references are unrevised recitals.

3. **11062 cites a dairy scope-modification proclamation that does not exist.** 11062 clause (3) says the dairy exclusion does not affect products “as further established in the Proclamation of September 8, 2026 (Modifying the Scope of Products of Canada ... With Respect to Dairy)” (91 FR 58320). No such dairy scope proclamation was published. The only September 8 dairy proclamation is 11062 itself; the analogous scope modifications exist only for alcoholic beverages (11064) and motor vehicles (11065).

4. **11064 and 11065 say the section 338 duties stack on section 232, contrary to the original proclamations.** 11046 clause (2) says the duties “shall not apply to articles subject to duties pursuant to section 232” (91 FR 46641), and 11048 clause (2) says the same (91 FR 46665). But 11064 clause (1) says the 11046 duties “shall apply in addition to duties imposed pursuant to section 232” (91 FR 58332), and 11065 clause (1) says the same for 11048 (91 FR 58340). The later scope clauses operate as amendments, but neither says it is amending clause (2).

5. **The June and July notices say all 60 economies failed both to impose and to enforce a ban, while also saying six failed only to enforce.** The June notice first divides 54 economies that “failed to impose and effectively enforce” from six that “failed to effectively enforce” (91 FR 34274), then says “all of the investigated economies have failed both to impose ... and to effectively enforce” (91 FR 34274).
