## The rules in brief

**Section 338 duty.** 50% ad valorem on listed products of Canada, "effective for goods entered for consumption, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after" the effective time (clause (1) of 11046/11047/11048). 11056 (1) reset that time to 12:01 a.m. ET August 22, 2026. None of the ten documents has an in-transit exception, a sunset date, or any text on USMCA goods.

**Section 232 interaction.** Clause (2) of 11046, 11047 and 11048: "The duties imposed in this proclamation shall not apply to articles subject to duties pursuant to section 232". 11064 (1), 11065 (1) and 11076 (1): the duties imposed pursuant to 11046 (or 11048), "as modified in this proclamation, shall apply in addition to duties imposed pursuant to section 232", for entries on or after 12:01 a.m. ET September 15, 2026 (October 30 for 11076, which restates it). 11047 was never modified, so the dairy duty keeps the section 232 carve-out. (Image: the HTSUS note edits in 11064 and 11065 confirm this; after September 15 only the dairy heading keeps the carve-out.)

**Import exclusions.** 11061/11062/11063 (1): Annex products "are excluded from importation into the United States, effective with respect to goods imported on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern time on September 29, 2026." Clause (2): products "that were imported, but not yet entered for consumption, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, prior to September 29, 2026, will remain subject to the 50 percent duty rate". The duty turns on entry or withdrawal; the exclusion turns on importation.

**Part A and Part B.** In 11064 and 11065, Annex I Part A products "shall be subject" and Part B products "shall no longer be subject". In 11076 the labels are reversed: Part A products "shall no longer be subject", Part B products "shall be subject".

**Section 301 rates** (memorandum sec. 1(a), 91 FR 47720; notice, 91 FR 47320–47321):

- 10% flat: Argentina, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Canada, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, India, Indonesia, Jordan, Malaysia, Mexico, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Trinidad and Tobago, United Kingdom.
- 10% net of MFN: European Union, Taiwan. If the MFN rate is below 10%, MFN + section 301 = 10%; if it is 10% or more, the section 301 duty is zero.
- 12.5% net of MFN: Japan, South Korea, Switzerland. Same rule with 12.5%.
- 12.5% flat: the other 38 investigated economies (including Norway, Türkiye, Hong Kong, Brazil, Thailand, Israel, the Dominican Republic).
- Not among the 60 economies: no duty under this action (for example Liechtenstein).

Flat rates are added to the MFN duty (note 52(a)). For a specific or compound MFN rate of an EU, Japanese, South Korean, Swiss or Taiwanese good, the ad valorem equivalent is the duty payable under column 1-General divided by the customs value (note 52(k), 91 FR 47394).

**Section 301 timing** (DATES, 91 FR 47318): entries or warehouse withdrawals "on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern time on July 24, 2026, except that goods loaded onto a vessel at the port of loading and in transit on the final mode of transit before 12:01 a.m. eastern time on July 24, 2026, and entered for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption before 12:01 a.m. eastern time on July 28, 2026, shall not be subject to such additional duty."
